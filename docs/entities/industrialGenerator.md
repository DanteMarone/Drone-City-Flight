# Industrial Generator

A heavy-duty diesel generator for industrial zones, construction sites, and backup power scenarios.

## Visuals
The generator is constructed from a composition of primitives:
- **Base**: A steel skid made of I-beams (BoxGeometry) with dark metal material.
- **Enclosure**: A main housing box in industrial yellow/green, featuring chamfered details and access panels.
- **Cooling**: A front-mounted radiator box with a recessed grille and internal fan blades.
- **Exhaust**: A vertical exhaust stack with a bent pipe and a hinged weather flap that vibrates.
- **Control Panel**: A rear-mounted electrical box with an emissive status screen and buttons.

## Animation
- **Fan**: The internal cooling fan spins continuously.
- **Exhaust Flap**: The weather cap on the exhaust pipe vibrates randomly to simulate engine rumble and exhaust pressure.
- **Status Light**: A small green indicator light on the control panel blinks steadily to indicate operation.

## Parameters
- `color`: (Optional) Hex color for the main enclosure. Default is Industrial Yellow (`0xdcb439`).
